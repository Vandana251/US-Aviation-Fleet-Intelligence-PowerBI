3. MISSING VALUES & BUSINESS MEANING HANDLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Flight Times & Delays (dep_time, arr_time, dep_delay, arr_delay, taxi_out, taxi_in, wheels_off, wheels_on, air_time, actual_elapsed_time):
  * Kept as empty/null for Cancelled and Diverted flights.
  * Rationale: Flights that did not operate or did not arrive at destination do not have valid flight durations or timestamps. Replacing with 0 would artificially skew arithmetic averages and flight metrics.
- Delay Breakdown Columns (carrier_delay, weather_delay, nas_delay, security_delay, late_aircraft_delay):
  * Missing/null values cleanly imputed to 0.
  * Valid zero values are strictly preserved (0 delay minutes means zero delay from that source).
- Cancellation Code (cancellation_code):
  * For non-cancelled flights, empty strings represent 'Not Cancelled'.
  * For cancelled flights, codes A, B, C, D are preserved and translated in DelayReason.
- Flight Number (op_carrier_fl_num):
  * Handled 1 missing flight number as an empty string, cleaned float formatting (.0 removed).
- Scheduled Elapsed Time (crs_elapsed_time):
  * 1 missing record preserved as null.

4. INVALID & SPECIAL VALUES HANDLED

- Negative Departure and Arrival Delays:

- Zero Delays:
  * Kept intact representing on-time operations.

5. NEW DERIVED COLUMNS CREATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Route (Text)
   - Combination of Origin and Destination airport IATA codes (e.g. "JFK-LAX").
2. MonthName (Text)
   - Full English month name ("January" to "December") for clean time-series visualization.
3. DayOfWeekName (Text)
   - Full English day of week name ("Monday" to "Sunday") for day-level pattern analysis.
4. TotalDelayMinutes (Float)
   - Total attributable delay minutes calculated from delay causes and positive arrival delay.
5. DelayStatus (Text)
   - Categorical classification: 'Early', 'On Time', 'Delayed' (arr_delay >= 15 min per FAA standard), 'Cancelled', 'Diverted'.
6. DelayReason (Text)
   - Granular root-cause categorization:
     * 'Carrier Delay' (within airline control: maintenance, crew, cleaning, baggage, fueling)
     * 'Late Aircraft Delay' (ripple delay from previous leg)
     * 'National Aviation System Delay' (ATC, airport congestion, ground delay programs)
     * 'Weather Delay' (extreme meteorological conditions)
     * 'Security Delay' (security breach or screening delay)
     * 'Cancelled - Carrier', 'Cancelled - Weather', 'Cancelled - NAS', 'Cancelled - Security'
     * 'Diverted'
     * 'No Delay / On Time'

6. DATASET LIMITATIONS & ASSUMPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Maintenance Specifics:
  * The BTS reporting standard aggregates maintenance delays inside 'Carrier Delay' (and cancellation code 'A').
  * The dataset does not contain individual aircraft tail numbers, aircraft models (e.g., B737, A320), or specific mechanical work orders.
  * Maintenance tracking in Power BI should focus on Carrier Delay patterns and Carrier Cancellations as the operational maintenance envelope.
